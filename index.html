Setup

Runs in Chrome, Edge, Brave, or Firefox.

01

Install a userscript manager

Add Tampermonkey (or Violentmonkey) to your browser.

02

Install the bot script

Open the GreasyFork distribution link and confirm installation in Tampermonkey.

03

Open your Reposts and run

Navigate to your TikTok profile, unhide or open the Reposts tab, click the first video, and press Start Auto-Unrepost.

Questions

Is my account safe from bans? +

Zero bans have been reported. The script randomizes click intervals and inserts 10–15 second breaks every 15–25 posts. For best results, cap sessions at 100–200 removals per day.

Does it access my password or login tokens? +

No. It executes locally inside your active browser tab using standard DOM interactions. No credentials, cookies, or session tokens are ever read or transmitted.

Why is the Reposts tab missing on desktop? +

TikTok hides the Reposts tab on desktop browsers by default. Use the Unhide Reposts Tab button inside the floating panel to reveal it immediately.
