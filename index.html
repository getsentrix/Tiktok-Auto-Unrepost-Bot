Frequently asked questions

Will this get my TikTok banned?
No accounts have been banned. The bot takes randomized pauses every 15–25 posts to keep behavior natural. For safety, clear 100–200 posts per day.
Does it need my TikTok password?
Never. It runs completely inside your browser tab on your already logged-in session. It never asks for, sends, or touches your password.
Why can't I see my Reposts tab on desktop?
TikTok hides the tab by default on computers. Click the script's "Unhide Reposts Tab" button and it pops right back up.
