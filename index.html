WHY IT WORKS

Built to Work When TikTok Updates

TikTok changes their website design all the time. This script looks for actual buttons and text instead of breaking every week.

Skips Photo Slideshows

Never gets stuck flipping sideways through photos. It recognizes photo albums and scrolls down to the next video properly.

Safe Delays & Pauses

Automatically takes a quick break every 15 to 25 videos with human-like delays so TikTok won't flag or temporarily lock your account.

Checks Before Counting

Checks that the removal notification actually showed up before counting it and moving to the next post. No fake numbers.

Works in Your Language

Works in English, Spanish, French, German, Russian, Portuguese, Turkish, Polish, and Italian without having to change any settings.

Unhides Your Reposts Tab

TikTok likes to hide the Reposts tab on computer browsers. One click on "Unhide Reposts Tab" brings it right back so you can open it.

100% Free & Open Source

No logins, no passwords, and no weird permissions. Everything runs straight in your own browser tab and you can read the code anytime.
